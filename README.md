# Music Portfolio

A responsive React portfolio for an electronic producer and creative.

## Personalize it

1. Update `PROFILE` near the top of `src/App.jsx` with your full name, email, location, and social links.
2. Confirm the genre labels in the `tracks` array. All eight supplied excerpts are already converted to web-ready AAC files in `public/audio/`.
3. The five supplied photographs and artworks live in `public/images/`; the optimized visualizer lives in `public/video/`.
4. The supplied résumé is available at `public/documents/trey-may-music-resume.pdf`.
5. The Opium research deck and its web cover are stored in `public/documents/` and `public/images/`.
6. Review the Future Club case study language and change “in development” once the campaign is live.
